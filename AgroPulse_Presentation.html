Project Overview & Thesis

AgroPulse: AI-Based Precision Fertilizer Recommendation

A smart farming system that combines a soil moisture sensor with Google Gemini Vision AI to tell farmers exactly how much water and fertilizer to use.

⚡ WHAT THE PROJECT DOES

AgroPulse is an AI-powered precision farming platform that combines real-time soil data from IoT sensors with advanced AI from Google Gemini and Grok Vision trained on massive multimodal datasets. It provides ICAR-calibrated fertilizer recommendations in safe, practical doses that farmers can use directly in the field.

PART 1 · THE SENSOR

Checking Soil Moisture

A hardware probe placed in the soil measures water percentage and air temperature in real time, so we know if the ground is dry, healthy, or waterlogged.

PART 2 · THE AI VISION

Scanning the Leaf

A phone camera photo is analyzed by Google Gemini AI to identify the plant type and spot visible problems like yellow leaf edges, brown dead spots, or fungal mold.

PART 3 · THE RECIPE

The Exact Fertilizer Recipe

Translates agricultural formulas into safe, everyday measurements—how many buckets of water and how many spoonfuls of fertilizer—so farmers never burn their crops.

The Core Problem

The Deadly Guesswork: Why Yellow Leaves Trick Farmers

Looking at leaf color alone is dangerous. Two plants can look identical on top, but have completely opposite problems in the soil.

THE DEADLY PARADOX

Two Leaves Look Identical. One Needs Water, The Other Will Drown.

CASE 1 · THIRSTY PLANT (DRY SOIL: 18%)

Yellow Margins + Dry Ground

The roots lack water, making leaves turn yellow. The plant needs water first so fertilizer can be safely dissolved without burning the roots.

CASE 2 · DROWNING PLANT (SOAKED SOIL: 82%)

Yellow Margins + Waterlogged Ground

Roots are suffocating in mud, so they cannot absorb nutrients. Adding chemical fertilizer will burn roots and kill the plant within 48 hours.

⚠️ Camera-only phone apps only look at the yellow leaf and tell farmers to add chemicals—accidentally killing drowning plants.
68%
Chemical Over-Dosing Rate

Farmers over-apply synthetic Urea due to panic and guesswork, wasting money on unneeded chemicals.

18 Days
Soil Testing Lab Delay

Government soil testing laboratories take weeks to return results—far too slow for sick plants.

4.4 pH
Soil Acidification Danger

Dumping excess chemical fertilizer destroys good soil microbes and damages long-term crop yield.

The Solution: Checking the Leaf & the Soil Together

ABOVE GROUND · THE CAMERA'S JOB

What Google Gemini AI Sees

  • ✓
    Identifies the Plant

    Recognizes Tomato, Potato, Maize, Chilli, Mango, and other regional crops automatically.

  • ✓
    Spots Leaf Problems

    Detects yellowing edges, brown dead spots, powdery white mold, and leaf rot.

  • ✓
    Rejects Invalid Photos

    Automatically blocks photos of faces, shoes, pets, or indoor objects to keep advice accurate.

BELOW GROUND · THE SENSOR'S JOB

What the Soil Sensor Measures

  • ✓
    Soil Moisture Percentage (%)

    Directly reads how wet or dry the soil is around the root zone.

  • ✓
    Air Temperature & Humidity

    Checks temperature (°C) and air moisture (% RH) with an integrated DHT11 sensor.

  • ✓
    Fungus Risk Warning

    Warns if muggy, humid weather will cause mold to grow after watering.

THE GOLDEN RULE How the System Decides:
If soil is dry (< 25%) → Water First, Then Feed | If soil is soaked (> 70%) → Stop Watering, Don't Add Fertilizer

OFFLINE SAFETY · 2 WAYS IT RUNS

How AgroPulse Works in Remote Fields (No Internet)

Mode 1 · Connected Mode (Cloud AI): When internet is available, leaf photos are analyzed by Google Gemini AI for complete disease identification.
Mode 2 · Remote Field Mode (Offline Sensor): When there is no internet, the phone connects directly to the ESP32 sensor. A built-in offline rules engine reads soil moisture and gives instant emergency watering advice with zero cloud connection.
